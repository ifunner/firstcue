Privacy

Your plan stays with you

FirstCue has no accounts and no database. Here is exactly where your answers go.

What’s stored, and where

  • On your device. Your planner (names, wedding date, songs, notes) is saved in this browser’s local storage so you can come back to it. It never goes to a FirstCue server, because there isn’t one.
  • Inside your links. Planner links and plan links carry your whole plan, compressed, in the part after the #. Browsers never send that part to the website, so the site host (GitHub Pages) doesn’t see it.
  • In files you download. Backup files, text summaries and spreadsheets stay wherever you save them.

Who can see your plan

Anyone you send a link or file to. Treat your planner link like a shared document link: send it to your partner, planner and DJ, not to a public group.

Other services we talk to

  • Song names from links. When you paste a Spotify, YouTube or Apple Music link, FirstCue asks Spotify (open.spotify.com) or noembed.com for the song’s name. Only that one song link is sent, never your names or the rest of your plan. If the lookup doesn’t work, you can type the name yourself.
  • Sharing. WhatsApp, Email and Share buttons hand your message to that app. What happens next is up to that app.
  • Fonts and code are served from this site itself. There are no ads, no trackers and no cookies.

Deleting your data

Clearing this site’s data in your browser settings removes everything FirstCue saved on this device. DJs can also remove a couple from Your couples on the home page. Links and files you’ve already sent stay with whoever has them.
